Introduction

There is a growing rift or tense standoff between an independent Chris and the party Trombly as to wether he should be enabled to build & sell liquid cooled computers for a living.

DLC Abbreviation

DLC stands for DemonsLoveCult, an obvious nod to video game culture.

The parent company DLC has a myriad of other business concepts that follow the same naming convention. For example:

  • DLCalendar
  • DLComputers
  • DLCars
  • DLCafe
  • and so on...

DLComputer Explainer

A Demons Love Computer is meant to be the holy grail | holy mary of all computer builds. These aren't meant to be affordable builds sold en masse.

Each DLCare package of a Demons Liquid Cooled build is built around a custom theme:

  • Video Game IP
  • Artist
  • Sports Team | Player
  • Movie

Wu-Tang Clan is a great example of a theme to build a DLC around. There is a strong affiliation with the colors black & yellow. The DLComputer is likely to be black & yellow. The DLCare package should include as much merch ( vinyls, cds, t-shirts, hoodies, signed & limited edition gear ) that can be included.

It's a superfans package. It might be possible someday to build in sets of two; 1 for sale and 2 for charity.
